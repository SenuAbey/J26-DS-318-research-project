Limitations

One store (CA_1), 129 products, weekly data.
Results may differ for other stores, products or daily data. PP2 will test the method on factory data.

Lower bound is weak (often 0); the upper bound drives ordering.
Demand cannot go below 0 and many products sell few units, so the lower bound adds little. The order rule uses the upper bound.

Order costs are assumed (shortage = 4 × holding), not real factory costs.
M5 has no cost data, so the cost savings are a scenario. Real costs will replace them in PP2.
